How Selling Your House Stops Foreclosure

When you sell your house to Brevard Cash Buyers, the proceeds pay off your mortgage lender before the foreclosure auction. This stops the foreclosure process completely, protects your credit score from further damage, and allows you to walk away with any remaining equity.

Unlike a foreclosure — which destroys your credit for 7 years and leaves you with nothing — a cash sale lets you control the timeline and keep what's yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell my house if I'm already in foreclosure?

Yes. You have the legal right to sell your property up until the auction date. The proceeds from the sale pay off your lender, and any remaining equity is yours. This is called a "pre-foreclosure sale."

How fast can you really close?

We can close in as little as 7 days. Typical timeline: Day 1 — you call us, Day 2 — property visit, Day 3 — cash offer, Day 7-10 — close at title company. If your auction is sooner, we may be able to expedite further.

Will selling stop the foreclosure on my credit report?

Selling your house before the auction prevents a foreclosure from being completed and recorded. While late payments may still appear, avoiding a completed foreclosure saves your credit score from the worst damage (which can drop your score 100-160 points).

What if I owe more than my house is worth?

If you owe more than your home's current value, we may still be able to help through a short sale negotiation with your lender. We've successfully negotiated short sales with many Brevard County lenders. Call us to discuss your specific situation.
