## 6. Image Optimization Plan

### 6.1 Critical Images to Compress
14 images on `/our-projects/` plus 1 on `/sell-your-house/` exceed 100 KB. The largest is `backyard-after-1.png` at **1.63 MB**.

### 6.2 Compression Targets

| Format | Current State | Target | Method |
|--------|--------------|--------|--------|
| JPG | 100–634 KB | < 80 KB | Quality 75–80%, WebP conversion |
| PNG (photos) | 400–1,630 KB | < 150 KB | Convert to WebP or compress PNG |
| PNG (icons) | 20–80 KB | < 30 KB | Keep PNG, run through TinyPNG |

### 6.3 Recommended Plugin Stack
- **ShortPixel** — bulk optimization, WebP generation, adaptive images
- **NitroPack** — already installed; verify image lazy loading is on
- **Cloudflare** — if using Pro, enable Polish for automatic WebP
