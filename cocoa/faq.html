General Questions

How fast can I sell my house in Cocoa, FL?

You can sell your Cocoa house for cash in as little as 7-14 days. We provide a fair cash offer within 24 hours of viewing your property and can close on your timeline.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No. We buy houses as-is in any condition. You don't need to clean, repair, or stage the property. We handle everything after purchase.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. There are zero realtor commissions, zero closing costs, and no hidden fees. The cash offer we present is exactly what you receive at closing.

What types of properties do you buy?

We buy all types of residential properties in Cocoa including single-family homes, condos, townhomes, duplexes, and multi-family properties. We purchase houses in any condition.

How do you determine the cash offer?

Our offers are based on current Cocoa market conditions, comparable sales in your neighborhood, your property's condition, and necessary repairs. We provide transparent, no-obligation offers.

What areas do you buy in?

We buy houses throughout Brevard County including Cocoa, Cocoa Beach, Melbourne, Palm Bay, Titusville, Satellite Beach, Merritt Island, Rockledge, Viera, and all surrounding areas.

Situation-Specific Questions

Can I sell during a divorce?

Yes. We work with both parties and their attorneys for a fast, fair cash sale. The title company can split proceeds at closing per your agreement.

Can I sell a rental property with tenants?

Yes. We buy tenant-occupied properties regularly. We'll honor existing leases or work out a transition plan. You don't need to evict anyone before selling.

Can I sell if I'm relocating out of state?

Yes. We handle remote sales through virtual walkthroughs, electronic signatures, and wire transfers. You never have to come back to Florida.

Do you buy estate properties?

Yes. We specialize in helping heirs sell estate properties quickly. We understand estate complexities and can work with executors and multiple heirs.

What if my house has code violations or liens?

We regularly purchase properties with code violations, tax liens, and title issues. Our team works directly with the City of Cocoa and Brevard County to resolve these at closing.

Can I sell if I owe more than it's worth?

If you owe more than your home's current value, we may be able to help through a short sale negotiation with your lender. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
