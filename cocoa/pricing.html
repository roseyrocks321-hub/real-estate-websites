How We Calculate Our Cash Offers

1. After-Repair Value (ARV)

We look at what similar homes in your Cocoa neighborhood have sold for in the last 6 months in move-in ready condition.

2. Repair Costs

We estimate what it will cost us to bring the property to market condition — roof, HVAC, kitchen, flooring, paint, etc.

3. Our Costs

We factor in closing costs, holding costs during renovation, and a reasonable profit margin for the risk we take on.

4. Your Offer

ARV minus repairs minus our costs = your cash offer. We show you the math. No hidden numbers.

When Does a Cash Sale Make More Sense?

  • Your house needs major repairs — Foundation, roof, mold, hoarding. Traditional buyers will ask for concessions or walk away.
  • You have a tight deadline — Every week matters. A cash sale moves fast.
  • You inherited the property — Out-of-state heirs often net more by selling fast than paying months of taxes, insurance, and utilities.
  • You're relocating — Paying two mortgages for 3 months wipes out most of the extra money from a traditional sale.
  • You need certainty — 1 in 3 traditional sales fall through due to financing issues. Cash sales close 99% of the time.
