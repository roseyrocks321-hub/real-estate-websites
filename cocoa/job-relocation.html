The Relocation Problem No One Talks About

You got the transfer. Great news — except now you need to sell your Cocoa home in 30 days. The corporate relocation package covers some moving costs, but it doesn't cover:

  • The stress of listing while packing boxes
  • Leaving your house vacant while it sits on the market
  • Paying a mortgage + rent in your new city
  • Coordinating showings from 1,000 miles away
  • The risk of a buyer's financing falling through after you've already moved
  • Managing repairs and inspections remotely
Every month your house sits unsold costs you roughly $1,500–3,000 in mortgage, taxes, insurance, and utilities. On a 3-month listing, that's $4,500–9,000 in holding costs — before you even pay realtor commissions.

How We Handle Out-of-State Sellers

Many of our sellers have already moved by the time they contact us. Here's how we make it work:

1. Virtual Property Review

Send us photos and a video walkthrough — or we can access the property if you've left a key with a neighbor or property manager. No need to fly back to Florida.

2. Electronic Everything

Purchase agreement, disclosures, closing docs — all signed electronically through DocuSign. You can complete everything from your new home.

3. Remote Closing

Florida allows remote online notarization (RON). You can notarize closing documents via video call. Or, a mobile notary can come to your new location.

4. Direct Deposit

Proceeds are wired directly to your bank account. No waiting for a check in the mail.

Frequently Asked Questions — Relocation Home Sales

Can you close before my move date?

Yes. We can close in as little as 7-10 days. If you give us your move date, we'll work backward to close with time to spare.

What if I'm already living in another state?

No problem. We handle remote sales regularly. Virtual walkthroughs, electronic signatures, and wire transfers mean you never have to come back to Florida.

Do I need to clean or move everything out?

No. Leave what you don't want. We buy as-is, which means you can leave behind furniture, unwanted items, even trash. We handle cleanup.

Will you lowball me because I'm in a hurry?

No. Our offers are based on comparable sales and condition — not your timeline. Many sellers net more with a cash sale because they avoid holding costs, commissions, and repair expenses.

Can I close after I move if I need more time?

Absolutely. Get your offer now to lock in the price, then close whenever works for you — whether that's next week or next month.

What paperwork do I need from my employer?

None. We don't need relocation paperwork or employer verification. This is a direct cash sale between you and us. Your employer doesn't need to be involved.
